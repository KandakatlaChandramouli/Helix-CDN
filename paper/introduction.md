
# Introduction

Modern content delivery systems and large-scale distributed platforms must process millions of events while maintaining low latency and predictable performance.

Helix-CDN was developed as an experimental runtime platform to investigate event-driven architectures, scheduler design, runtime batching, memory management, and scalable network processing.

The project originated from studying large-scale streaming platforms and distributed infrastructure systems. The goal was not to reproduce production infrastructure, but to understand the engineering tradeoffs involved in building such systems from first principles.
